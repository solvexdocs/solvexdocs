# Why Solvex?

- **Decentralized Intelligence:** Combines AI accuracy with community wisdom for robust decision-making.
- **Reputation-Driven:** Influence in the network is earned through consistent, high-quality participation.
- **Solana-Powered:** Leverages Solana’s high throughput (65,000 TPS) and low transaction fees (~$0.00025 per transaction) for fast, cost-effective operations.
- **Innovative Consensus:** VPoS redefines governance by focusing on virtual actions (validation, reputation) rather than resource-intensive staking or mining.
