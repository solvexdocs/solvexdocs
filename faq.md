# FAQ

**Do I need to stake SOL to participate?**  
No, Solvex uses $SOLVEX for staking, not SOL, lowering the entry barrier.

**How does AI prevent manipulation?**  
The AI layer is transparent, with reports stored on-chain. Community validation ensures no single entity can manipulate outcomes.

**What happens if I validate incorrectly?**  
Incorrect validations reduce your RepScore, and repeated errors may lead to temporary penalties.

**Can I submit proposals anonymously?**  
Yes, but proposals must be submitted via a Solana wallet, ensuring traceability without revealing personal identity.

**How secure is Solvex?**  
Solvex leverages Solana’s secure blockchain and immutable records, with AI and community safeguards to prevent abuse.
