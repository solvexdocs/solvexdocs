# Roadmap and Future

## Short-Term Goals (2025-2026)

- **Q2 2025:** Launch Solvex mainnet on Solana.
- **Q3 2025:** Integrate advanced AI models for enhanced proposal analysis.
- **Q4 2025:** Partner with DAOs and DeFi projects for pilot use cases.

## Long-Term Vision (2027+)

- Expand to cross-chain validations (e.g., Ethereum, Polkadot).
- Develop a marketplace for validated projects, connecting startups with investors.
- Introduce zero-knowledge proofs for privacy-preserving validations.
