# Use Cases

## 1. DAO Governance

**Problem:** DAOs struggle with low participation and uninformed voting.  
**Solution:** Solvex enables AI-assisted proposal analysis and reputation-weighted voting, ensuring informed decisions.  
**Example:** A DAO validates a new governance rule, with validators earning $SOLVEX for participation.

## 2. Due Diligence for Startups

**Problem:** Investors lack reliable vetting for IDO/ICO projects.  
**Solution:** Solvex validates project whitepapers and teams, providing a trust score.  
**Example:** A startup submits its pitch, and validators confirm its legitimacy, attracting funding.

## 3. Information Validation

**Problem:** Misinformation spreads in decentralized ecosystems.  
**Solution:** Solvex validates news or research, ensuring credibility.  
**Example:** A news article is submitted for validation, with AI and community confirming its accuracy.

## 4. DeFi Audit

**Problem:** DeFi protocols face security risks from unaudited smart contracts.  
**Solution:** Solvex facilitates community-driven audits, with validators reviewing code.  
**Example:** A DeFi project submits its contract, and validators identify vulnerabilities, earning rewards.
