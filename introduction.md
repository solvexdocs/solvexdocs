# Introduction

## Overview of Solvex

Solvex is a decentralized protocol designed to facilitate community-driven validation of ideas, projects, and decisions using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Solana blockchain. By introducing the Virtual Proof of Solana (VPoS) consensus mechanism, Solvex enables scalable, secure, and efficient governance and validation processes without relying on traditional Proof of Work (PoW) or Proof of Stake (PoS) models.

## Mission

To empower communities to collaboratively validate and govern decentralized ecosystems by combining AI-driven insights with reputation-based human validation, ensuring trust, transparency, and scalability.
