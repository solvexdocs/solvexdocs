# Comparison with Analogues

| Feature              | Solvex (VPoS)        | PoS (e.g., Ethereum) | PoW (e.g., Bitcoin)    |
|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Energy Efficiency    | High (no mining)     | Moderate (staking)   | Low (energy-intensive) |
| Entry Barrier        | Low (no SOL staking) | High (requires staking) | High (requires hardware) |
| Scalability          | High (Solana-based)  | Moderate (post-upgrade) | Low (limited TPS)      |
| Consensus Speed      | Fast (sub-second finality) | Moderate (seconds)   | Slow (minutes)         |
| AI Integration       | Yes (risk analysis)  | No                   | No                     |
