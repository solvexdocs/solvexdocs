# Using Solvex

## How to Propose an Idea

1. **Connect Wallet:** Use a Solana-compatible wallet (e.g., Phantom, Solflare).
2. **Access Solvex dApp:** Visit the Solvex platform (URL TBD).
3. **Submit Proposal:**
   - Enter details (title, description, category).
   - Pay a $SOLVEX fee (e.g., 10 $SOLVEX).
4. **Monitor Status:** Track AI analysis and community validation on-chain.

## How to Participate in Validation

1. **Stake $SOLVEX:** Deposit tokens to become a validator.
2. **Browse Proposals:** Review open proposals and AI reports.
3. **Vote:** Use your Solana wallet to validate or reject proposals.
4. **Earn Rewards:** Receive $SOLVEX and RepScore updates after finalization.

## How to Earn $SOLVEX

- **Validate Proposals:** Earn rewards for accurate votes.
- **Submit Quality Proposals:** High-quality submissions may yield bonuses.
- **Stake $SOLVEX:** Higher stakes increase reward potential.

## Example Scenarios

- **DAO Governance:** A DAO uses Solvex to validate a treasury allocation proposal, ensuring community consensus.
- **IDO Due Diligence:** An IDO project submits its whitepaper for validation, gaining credibility if approved.
- **DeFi Audit:** A DeFi protocol requests a smart contract audit, with validators verifying code integrity.
