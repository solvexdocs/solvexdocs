# Why Solana?

- **High Throughput:** Supports 65,000 TPS, ideal for large-scale validations.
- **Low Fees:** Average transaction cost of $0.00025, making participation affordable.
- **Ecosystem:** Rich DeFi and NFT ecosystem, perfect for Solvex use cases.
- **Proof of History:** Enhances VPoS by providing a decentralized clock for transaction ordering.
